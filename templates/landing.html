Security First

Built for enterprise-grade protection

Every layer of this system is hardened against common attacks. Your team's data is protected from the ground up.

🔐

Bcrypt Password Hashing

Passwords are never stored in plain text. Industry-standard hashing with salting is used on every account.

🛡️

CSRF Token Protection

Every form submission is validated with a unique server-side token to prevent cross-site request forgery.

⏰

Auto Account Lockout

After 5 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes. Brute-force attacks are blocked automatically.

📌

Security Headers

HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, and more are set on every response to protect against XSS, clickjacking, and injection.
